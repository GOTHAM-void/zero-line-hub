Questions Clinics Ask

Tap any clinic bottleneck to see how PlusFlow resolves it

How do I stop my waiting room from getting overcrowded? +

Patients check in via reception or your desk QR standee and receive a live WhatsApp token link. They wait at home and walk in 10 minutes before their turn.

How does PlusFlow stop unpaid medicine leakage at the pharmacy? +

Your pharmacy packs the bag as soon as you finish the consult, but the handover button stays locked until the Front Desk marks the combined bill paid.

What if a patient has a drug allergy I might miss during a rush? +

Allergies stay saved permanently on the patient's Cloud ID. If you select a conflicting drug or salt, PlusFlow blocks it immediately before printing.

How fast can I write a prescription without typing everything? +

Use 1-click specialty templates for common cases (fever, URTI, BP, gastritis) or press Alt+V to dictate medicines and dosages directly into the Rx.

Can Front Desk, Doctor, and Pharmacy stay in sync on separate devices? +

Yes. Every check-in, prescription, stock deduction, and payment syncs in real time across desktop PCs, tablets, and phones with role-based access.

Is patient data kept private and safe from unauthorized staff or visitors? +

Yes. Production-Safe Mode clears patient data on idle lock, public tracker links expose zero personal details, and every clinical action is audit-logged.
